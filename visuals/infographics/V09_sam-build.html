The honest reading

$179M SAM supports a real company; it does not support a generational one. The MSP is the wedge, not the market. Internal IT is ~$288M more and this pack does not add it to the headline — entering it means competing on resolution quality, the axis we chose to avoid. Summing them into a $470M figure is the move a skeptical reader is looking for, and it is not made.
