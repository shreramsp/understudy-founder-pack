The failure mode this journey exists to prevent

Reverse beats 3 and 4 — show him a black box, answer the RPA question with a slogan — and Ray tells the team channel it is RPA with a chatbot on it. He is listened to, because he cleaned up after the last two. Capture then degrades quietly: sessions get paused, narration stops, and the library learns only the easy procedures. No dashboard shows this. It is the single most likely way this product dies inside a customer that has already paid.
