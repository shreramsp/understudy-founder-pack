Ray's veto has no detector

Every other row fails loudly. Ray's failure mode is disuse — he narrates less, pauses more, and the library quietly learns only the easy procedures. It presents as slow adoption. Experiment E6 exists solely to instrument it, and it runs for free inside E1.

Sonia recurs, which makes her a throughput constraint

One review per client environment at ~6 weeks means expansion velocity is set by review capacity, not sales capacity. That is why the revenue ramp is paced the way it is, and why the business-model hypothesis tests review cycle time rather than sales touches.
