The gap we are not hiding

Priced against the engineer-hours it displaces, that schedule does not hold. A consistent calculation puts the fee at 99–466% of the hours saved, depending on client size — above the value at every band. An hours-defensible schedule would blend to about $246 rather than $1,005.

We publish both because the question is genuinely open: is the library worth paying for on its own, or only worth the hours it saves? Ten conversations with MSP owners settle that, and no amount of modelling can. Until they happen, both numbers stand on this page.

One thing to plan for: the distillation node runs in your tenant, so raw recordings never leave your control. Hosted, that is roughly $150–250 a month — which we state here rather than in a later invoice.
