About

Why this problem, and what is missing.

Why now

Agents crossed a threshold in the last eighteen months: they can follow a multi-step workflow in a real interface and recover from a wrong click. That is the capability that turns a recording into something executable, and it did not exist in 2023. At the same time SKILL.md became an open format adopted across more than twenty platforms — so a distilled procedure finally has somewhere to live that is not a fine-tuned model.

This was tried before. A company called FortressIQ was recording employees with computer vision from 2017 and was acquired into an automation vendor in 2021. Right input, wrong decade: there was nothing to send the recording to.

The gap in the team, named

The founder’s background is AI and agent engineering. The clustering problem — turning many messy recordings into one reliable skill — is the hard bet in this company and it is the founder’s ground.

There is no managed-services operating history here. Nobody on this side has run an MSP or felt the 2am onboarding before a Monday contract start. That is why the plan begins with ten conversations and no product pitch, why the first hire is an MSP-side operator rather than an engineer, and why design partners are asked for their timesheets rather than their enthusiasm.

What would make us stop

If skills synthesised in one environment execute correctly in another less than half the time, after three genuine attempts at the method, the core claim is false. We would publish that. The field has assumed this works for forty years without measuring it, and a clean negative result is the most useful thing we could contribute if the positive one does not arrive.
