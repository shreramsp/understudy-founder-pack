Evidence

There is no traction. Here is exactly what we do and do not know.

No pilot, no customer, no code. This page exists because a pack that hides that would not be worth reading.

Sourced

Onboarding a median client costs 25–40 engineer-hours[S21], banded
26% of MSPs cannot staff clients they could win[S33]
Computer-use agents: 12% (Apr 2024) → ~86% on OSWorld[S14][S15]
Those same agents fail ~80% of real workflows; long-horizon completion is 20.6%[S15][S16]
RPA: 30–50% of projects fail; maintenance reaches 60% of cost[S10][S11]
ServiceNow implementation runs 3–5× the annual licence[S20]

Assumed, and tagged as such

The endpoint-to-user ratio behind the market model. The share of an MSP queue that is automatable. The hours a median client actually takes. The cost of the tickets we address. Each carries an (assumption) tag in the pack and names the experiment that would settle it.

Unknown, and stated

  • Whether skills transfer between environments at all. No benchmark exists, no prior system has published one, and it is the assumption the company rests on.
  • Whether an MSP owner pays for a library, or only for the hours it saves.
  • Whether a vendor founder can even join the channels this plan depends on.
  • Whether an abstracted pattern can be traced back to the tenant it came from.
Four of those five would end the company, and three of them cost under $10,000 to test. None needs code. That is the sequencing argument, and it is why the round is sized to fund a question rather than a company.
