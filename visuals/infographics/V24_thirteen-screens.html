UX specification · V24

Two screens decide adoption, and neither is a dashboard.

This product asks people to be recorded and asks a sceptical expert to endorse what was learned from them. Those are interface problems before they are model problems.

Cross-cutting rules

Skills render as text, never flowcharts — text is the format an expert can argue with
Every number monospaced and traceable to the session or action that produced it
Destructive actions visually distinct everywhere; never batchable, never defaulted
Nothing manufactures urgency — most days the correct action is none

Deliberately not built

NO A chat interface — it invites the unbounded requests P5 says we cannot serve
NO A confidence score — implies calibration we cannot demonstrate
NO An autonomy toggle — excluded by D1, not withheld
NO A contribution leaderboard — ranking the people being recorded gamifies capture
