Why the fourth quadrant stayed empty

Acting on an observed procedure required an executor that could work a real UI. Computer-use agents went from 12% to ~86% on OSWorld between April 2024 and September 2026, and SKILL.md became a portable cross-vendor format. FortressIQ recorded employees from 2017 with nowhere to send the output, and ended as a feature inside an RPA vendor. Same input, wrong decade.
