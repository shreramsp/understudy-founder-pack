Understudy is a founder-artifact pack generated by an artifact pipeline — not a funded company, not a shipping product, and not a company with customers. There is no traction: no pilot, no customer, no code. Every operating figure on this site is a declared target carrying its source or an (assumption) tag, and the pack states where each would be falsified. Row-by-row completeness is in audit/COVERAGE.md.
