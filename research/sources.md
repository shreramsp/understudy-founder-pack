> **What this is** — the numbered citation index for every fact in the research layer. Each entry carries what it supports and the date it was accessed.
> **Why it exists** — the pack's central discipline is that a number either traces to a source here or is tagged `(assumption)`. Without this file the discipline is unenforceable and the pack becomes assertion; with it, a skeptic can check any claim in one hop.
> **How to read it** — follow the `[Sn]` tag from wherever you met the claim. Note the authority column: vendor marketing and analyst benchmarks are not equal evidence, and several key numbers here are the former.

## Marked gaps — searched, not found

- **No public count of MSPs globally.** Directories list curated subsets (one lists 2,102 vetted providers [S18]); no authoritative census exists. `strategy/market_sizing.md` must build bottom-up from endpoint and spend data, not from a provider count.
- **No independent verification of any AI-ITSM vendor's deflection rate.** Every resolution-rate figure in this layer (Ravenna 35–60%, −70% ticket volume) is vendor-published [S1][S2]. No third-party benchmark of autonomous IT resolution was found.
- **No public pricing for Ravenna, Atomicwork, or Moveworks.** All are quote-based; the per-ticket comparison in `financials/pricing.md` must anchor on the HDI/MetricNet cost-per-ticket data [S27][S28] instead.
- **No authoritative tickets-per-user-per-month figure.** MetricNet defines the metric [S34] but no free, dated benchmark value was found; `strategy/market_sizing.md` carries 0.9/endpoint/month as an explicit `(assumption)` and shows the sensitivity band.
- **FortressIQ post-acquisition fate is undocumented.** No public teardown of what happened to the product or its customers after January 2022 — a gap that matters, because it is the closest historical analogue to this company.
