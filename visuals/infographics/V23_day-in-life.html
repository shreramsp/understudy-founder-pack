Why 14:20 matters more than the rest of the day

Verification passed. The system was not wrong about what it did — only about whether to do it, and no success criterion catches that. What contained it: the undo path recorded before the mutation (40-second revert), the agent's own principal (4-minute post-mortem instead of an afternoon), same-day disclosure, and the ticket not billing because a human reversed it.

This class of error is not eliminable, only survivable. A product claiming otherwise would be lying.
