Two corrections that produced this table

The earlier version computed hours displaced three different ways — crossed extremes for two bands, matched pairs for the third. That inconsistency is what produced D11's cascade, and under one method typical is the worst band, not complex — the reverse of what D11 acted on.

The rate was also wrong: $75/hr implied a $156k loaded technician, above the top of this file's own $95–120k range.

E3 is a basis test, not a price test

The fee is above displaced value at every band, at every point. So the question is not "will they pay $1,500" — it is whether an MSP owner pays for the library at all, or only for the hours it saves. Ten conversations distinguish three different companies: a $179M SAM, a $124M SAM, or a per-MSP platform fee.
