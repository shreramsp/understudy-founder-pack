What to track · V30

The vanity metrics here are unusually seductive.

A capture product generates impressive volume numbers from day one that are entirely uncorrelated with whether it works.

Track vs ignore

StageTrackIgnore, and why
DiscoveryUnprompted problem mentions ÷ 10 · onboarding hours by client size · ticket mix from real exports · earlyvangelist criteria met (of 5)Interviews conducted · positive sentiment · pipeline size — a pipeline failing criterion 4 is the most flattering and least predictive thing a founder can build
ValidationCross-environment transfer rate · skills endorsed by their author · hours vs the client's size band · verified-action accuracy · security review cycle timeSessions captured · skills generated — a library can grow while getting worse; over-merging produces more skills, not fewer · aggregate hours saved, which buries the environment-1 overrun
CreationEnvironments per MSP per quarter · reuse rate across environments · NRR post-backfill · reversal rateLogo count — one MSP with 41 environments outweighs four with three · gross revenue during backfill, which is one-time and reads like a trend
BuildingDistinct MSPs in the global layer (m≥3) · global-layer contribution to a new customer's first environment · published time-to-first-verified-skillEnvelope entries published · total environments covered · internal benchmark scores nobody can reproduce

Dangerous at every stage

Automation rate rises when the envelope is widened carelessly and when gates are loosened — both of which make the product worse. It is the number the category advertises and the one most likely to be optimised against the customer's interest.

Capture volume grows with the customer's headcount, is uncorrelated with whether clustering works, and — because it looks like traction — is the metric most likely to be reported to investors during the period when nothing is being learned.

The two that detect failures nothing else sees

endorsement ratePredicts churn earlier than any usage metric, and is the earliest visible signal of the expert's silent veto
reversal rateThe only detector for verified-but-wrong actions — the 14:20 class
